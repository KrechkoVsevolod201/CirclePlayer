# CirclePlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

CirclePlayer plays audio files stored on your Android device. It does not
require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or a service
that sends your music library to the developer.

## Access to files

The app requests access to audio files so it can list and play local music.
You can also choose a music folder or a Click Wheel sound through Android's
system file picker. The app reads the selected files on your device; it does
not upload, copy, or share them with the developer.

## Data stored on the device

App preferences such as the selected folder, interface settings, and saved
themes are stored locally. Android may include app settings in device backup
or device-to-device transfer according to your Android backup settings. Music
files themselves are not copied by CirclePlayer.

## Playback and permissions

Android's media session, notification, and connected media controls may show
the title, artist, and album artwork of the track currently being played.
This information is provided to Android and any media devices you connect.
The app uses vibration only when you enable Click Wheel vibration.

## Data sharing and deletion

CirclePlayer does not send personal data to the developer and does not share
data with third parties. You can remove locally stored settings by clearing
the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Persisted folder and sound access
can also be revoked in Android settings.
